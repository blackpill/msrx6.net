We aim to distinguish confirmed facts from compatibility guidance and third-party claims.

- Download pages identify when a file is hosted by a third party.
- We do not claim a checksum has been verified unless we publish the exact value and verification date.
- Compatibility information is reviewed against available product labels, app listings, and practical platform requirements.
- Material changes display a review date when appropriate.
- Corrections are prioritized when they affect download safety, compatibility, or responsible use.
- Content does not endorse unauthorized access, payment-card fraud, credential copying, or privacy violations.
