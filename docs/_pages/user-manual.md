## Identify your model

- **MSRX6 USB:** desktop connection through USB.
- **MSRX6BT:** USB plus Bluetooth support for compatible mobile apps.
- Record the exact label and hardware revision before requesting support.

## Basic setup

1. Inspect the device, cable, and card path for damage.
2. Install the appropriate [software](/software-download/).
3. Connect directly by USB, or follow the [Bluetooth guide](/bluetooth-software/).
4. Open the software and confirm the device status.
5. Test using clean, blank, or explicitly authorized media.
6. Swipe smoothly in the direction described by the device/vendor documentation.

## Care and storage

- Keep the card path free of dust and debris.
- Do not insert bent, wet, or damaged cards.
- Disconnect the device before cleaning.
- Store installers and configuration records in an access-controlled location.

## Responsible use

Use the device only for legitimate encoding, maintenance, access-control, loyalty, identification, or development workflows that you are authorized to perform. Follow applicable privacy, payment-security, and data-retention requirements.
